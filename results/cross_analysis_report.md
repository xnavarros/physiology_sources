# Cross-Analysis Subject Profiling Report

This report groups subjects into profiles based on the timing and location of their significant ERP and TFR effects.

## Profile: Co-localized Early Preparer

**Subjects:** BJ25, MC05

**Characteristics:**
- Shows significant ERP and TFR effects early in the preparatory period.
- Crucially, the brain regions of these early effects overlap.
- **Interpretation:** Strong evidence for a specific, localized preparatory strategy involving both slow potentials and oscillatory changes.

## Profile: Late/Motor-Focused Preparer

**Subjects:** JS08, MN23, SB27, TH24, VA14

**Characteristics:**
- TFR effects emerge primarily in the mid-to-late preparatory windows.
- Often associated with Beta band desynchronization in motor areas.
- **Interpretation:** Preparation seems to ramp up closer to the motor event.

## Profile: Mixed/Other

**Subjects:** VS06

**Characteristics:**
- This group shows a mix of effects that does not fit into a clear category.

## Profile: Reactive Responder

**Subjects:** LP26

**Characteristics:**
- Shows no significant effects during preparation.
- Effects only appear *after* the sigh is executed.
- **Interpretation:** Employs a reactive strategy with minimal neural preparation.

## Profile: Sustained ERP Responder (No specific TFR prep)

**Subjects:** OL04

**Characteristics:**
- Shows a widespread ERP difference throughout the trial.
- Lacks specific preparatory TFR effects.
- **Interpretation:** May reflect a general state of alertness or cognitive load without specific oscillatory motor preparation signals.
